Using FTP to Transfer Files
    File Transfer Protocol (FTP)

FTP clients require three pieces of information to connect to your web server; this information will have been sent to you by your hosting provider
after you set up your account:
. The hostname, or address, to which you will connect
. Your account username
. Your account password
